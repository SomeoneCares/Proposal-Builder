## Overview

Training and knowledge transfer are delivered as a standalone module within the base scope. Training is not contingent on taking the optional managed operations service — it is part of the base deliverable so that self-operation is a realistic choice.

Training is delivered remotely and covers platform administration, site provisioning, policy management, monitoring and log review, call flow administration, NVR administration and first-line troubleshooting. Documentation and a recorded session are provided for onward internal training in each location.

## Training Scope Notes

- Administration training covers platform administration, site provisioning, policy management, monitoring and log review, call flow administration, NVR administration and first-line troubleshooting.
- Documentation and a recorded session are provided for onward internal training in each location.
- Where the customer self-operates, the operational tooling and knowledge transfer are identical to what a managed-service customer receives — what differs is who sits in front of the console.
