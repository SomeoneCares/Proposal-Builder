## Process

When modifications, additions or edits to the scope are required, Verto Wave follows its established change management process. Both {{customer_short}} and Verto Wave can propose changes to the services, including deliverables, scope or any other aspect of the engagement. Changes may arise from actual experience, from {{customer_short}} clarifying or redefining its requirements, from either party not meeting its responsibilities, or from deviations from the assumptions, dependencies or scope stated in this proposal.

Every change request must be submitted in writing. This includes changes to plans, scope, specifications, schedules, designs, requirements, deliverables or software environments. Verto Wave is not obliged to undertake work that changes time, scope, cost or contractual obligations until both parties have agreed the change request in writing.

### Change Request Steps

1. **Collect information.** The requestor — {{customer_short}} or Verto Wave — notifies the Verto Wave primary contact with the details of the requested change. The primary contact prepares a preliminary change request form.
2. **Assess the impact.** Verto Wave assesses the effect of the change on deliverables, resources, budget and schedule, and its feasibility.
3. **Approve or reject.** Verto Wave decides whether to proceed with a full analysis, considering the overall context and stakeholder input. If the change affects the schedule or cost, Verto Wave obtains {{customer_short}}'s approval before the full analysis.
4. **Prioritize.** Verto Wave and {{customer_short}} prioritize change requests by urgency, impact and strategic importance.
5. **Execute and report.** Approved changes are implemented according to the agreed plan, and their status is included in the progress reports.

## Review and Approval

If both parties approve a full change request analysis, Verto Wave prepares a change request form with the justification, estimated cost, schedule and resource requirements, technical feasibility and a recommendation:

- Implementation without changing the current cost or delivery schedule
- Implementation with an impact on cost, delivery schedule or both
- A follow-on project

If Verto Wave determines that the change is not technically or economically feasible, it explains the reasons in writing.

Verto Wave reviews the change request form with {{customer_short}}. The form is then marked "accepted" or "withdrawn" and signed by both parties. An accepted change is incorporated into the services and invoicing is updated accordingly.
