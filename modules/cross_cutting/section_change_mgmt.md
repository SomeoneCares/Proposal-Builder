## Process

In the event that modifications, additions, or edits to the scope are required, Verto Wave will adhere to its established Change Management Process. Both the customer and Verto Wave have the authority to propose changes to the services, including deliverables, scope, or any other aspect of the project. Such changes may arise based on actual experience or as the customer clarifies or redefines their requirements. Additionally, modifications may be necessary if the customer does not fulfill their responsibilities or obligations outlined in this document, or if there are deviations from any assumptions, constraints, dependencies, or project scope specifications stated herein.

Any request for changes to the services must be submitted in writing. This includes requests for modifications to project plans, scope, specifications, schedules, designs, requirements, service deliverables, software environments, or any other aspect of this document. Verto Wave will not be obligated to undertake any tasks related to changes in time, scope, cost, or contractual obligations until both the customer and Verto Wave agree in writing to the proposed change request form through Verto Wave's authorized Change Management Process.

Verto Wave's change request process is a formalized approach to managing change requests within a project or organization. This process ensures that changes are evaluated, documented, and executed consistently.

### Change Request Process Steps

1. **Collect Information:** When a change is desired, the requestor — whether the customer or Verto Wave — will notify Verto Wave Primary Contact with the details of the requested change. The Primary Contact will then prepare a preliminary Project Change Request Form to outline the nature of the requested change.

2. **Impact Assessment:** Verto Wave will conduct an initial Impact Assessment to evaluate any potential positive or negative effects on project deliverables, resources, budget, and timelines. This assessment helps determine the feasibility of the change and its alignment with project goals.

3. **Approve or Reject Change Requests:** After assessment, Verto Wave will decide whether to approve or reject the change request. This decision will consider the overall project context, stakeholder input, and alignment with organizational objectives. If the change affects the services schedule or costs, Verto Wave will obtain customer approval before proceeding with a full analysis.

4. **Prioritize Change Requests:** Not all change requests hold the same significance. Verto Wave, in collaboration with the customer, will prioritize change requests based on urgency, impact, and strategic importance. Some changes may require immediate attention, while others can be scheduled for later implementation.

5. **Execution and Reporting:** Once approved, Verto Wave will implement the change according to the agreed-upon plan. The status of the Change Request will be included in the Progress Reports.

## Review and Approval

If both parties approve a full Change Request analysis, Verto Wave will prepare a Project Change Request Form detailing the change and its justification, directing the analysis effort to the appropriate resources. This analysis will result in a final Project Change Request Form that includes estimated costs, schedule and resource requirements, technical feasibility, and recommended actions such as:

- Implementation without adjusting current cost or delivery schedule.
- Implementation with an impact on customer costs, delivery schedule, or both.
- Recommendation for a follow-on project.

If Verto Wave determines that the project is not technically or economically feasible, a detailed explanation will be provided outlining the reasons for this assessment.

Verto Wave will review the Project Change Request Form with the customer. Following this review, it will be marked as "accepted" or "withdrawn," and both parties will sign the form. If marked as "accepted," Verto Wave will revise the services to incorporate the changes agreed upon, and Verto Wave's invoicing will be updated accordingly.
