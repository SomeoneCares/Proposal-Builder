## VertoWave Roles & Responsibilities

| Role | Responsibility |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Project Manager** | Oversee the project throughout its entire lifecycle. Collaborate with the project Tech Lead to create and maintain project plans as needed. Communicate project status to relevant stakeholders according to the VertoWave communication matrix. Monitor the overall project and RAID (Risks, Assumptions, Issues, Dependencies) status, addressing any issues and updating the RAID register as necessary. Ensure the final project sign-off by confirming all customer requirements are met according to the agreed-upon project scope. |
| **VertoWave Tech Lead** | Lead the VertoWave Build/Technical team throughout the project lifecycle. Engage in delivering technical and procedural activities to customers during various project stages. Participate in envisioning, planning, and design activities. Conduct solution requirements analysis through sessions and interviews with customer stakeholders. Develop design documents and ensure they are agreed upon with the customer. Provide technical consultation as needed throughout the project lifecycle. Conduct impact analysis and estimation for any change requests. Ensure successful system testing in accordance with UAT standards. Contribute to the final project sign-off to confirm all client requirements are met as per the approved project scope. Contribute to the delivery of technical and procedural activities to customers throughout various project stages. |
| **VertoWave Build / Consultancy / Implementation Team** | Contribute to the delivery of technical and procedural activities to customers throughout various project stages. Assist in conducting solution requirements analysis through sessions and interviews with customer stakeholders. Help create necessary design documents. Execute technical project activities. Collaborate in developing project documentation. Participate in the final project sign-off to ensure all client requirements are met as per the approved project scope. |
|| **VertoWave Project Management and Governance Office** | Supporting IT infrastructure projects. Providing governance, oversight, and standardized practices. Helping ensure that project stages are successful, deliver value, and align with the organization's strategic goals. |
| **Operations Team (L1/L2/L3)** | Provide operational support for in-scope systems and services per the OLA. L1: first-line incident handling, ticket logging, basic troubleshooting, escalation. L2: deeper troubleshooting, approved change/configuration implementation, ticket closure. L3: advanced issue resolution, vendor communication, strategic guidance on system improvements. |
| **Technical Account Manager** | Customer-facing role focusing on managing and supporting the technical aspects and relationship with the customer. |

## Customer Roles & Responsibilities

| Role | Responsibility |
| :--- | :--- |
| VertoWave Project Manager / Project Owner | Primary point of accountability on the customer side; coordinates stakeholders, approves deliverables, manages customer-side RAID inputs, supports timely feedback and decisions. |
| **Steering Committee / Executives Management / Middle Management** | Provides strategic direction, approves major scope/change decisions, resolves escalated issues, supports resource commitment. |
| **Customer Technical Teams (Team 1 … Team n)** | Provide environment information, access, and subject-matter support; participate in requirements gathering and design; perform customer-side operational tasks per the RACI; escalate issues as needed. |
| **Customer Stakeholders** | Dedicated participation in requirement gathering and system design; timely feedback and approvals to avoid schedule delays. |

## Communication & Reporting

- Project status reporting per the VertoWave communication matrix.
- RAID register updates throughout the project lifecycle.
- Monthly OLA/operations performance reports (where operations are in scope).
- Quarterly OLA/operations performance reviews (where operations are in scope).
- Change request submissions in writing per the Change Management Procedure.
- Customer to notify VertoWave Primary Contact of any change request, with details of the requested change.
