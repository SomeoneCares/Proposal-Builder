## Acceptance of Deliverables

Professional services deliverables are accepted against the acceptance criteria agreed during the designing and planning stage. <!-- if services -->
Licensed software is deemed delivered, and thereby accepted, on delivery of the keys or entitlements. <!-- if licenses -->
Managed services are not subject to acceptance; performance is measured and reported against the Operations Level Agreement. <!-- if managed_services -->
