## Phase 1 — Architecture

### In-scope

- Conduct {{services_arch_workshops}} architecture / envisioning workshops.
- Produce a high-level architecture design covering {{services_branches}} branches.
- Produce the monitoring architecture design covering {{services_apps_monitored}} applications and {{services_network_devices}} network devices.
- Produce the automation / orchestration framework design for {{services_workflows}} workflows.
- Produce the endpoint rollout architecture for {{services_endpoints}} endpoints.
- Produce the NVR architecture design for {{services_nvr_cameras}} cameras.
- Produce the IoT device integration architecture for {{services_iot_devices}} IoT devices.
- Produce the SD-WAN overlay topology design for {{services_tunnels}} tunnels.
- Produce the firewall policy architecture design for {{services_firewall_policies}} policies.
- Produce the PBX numbering-plan and call-flow design for {{services_pbx_extensions}} extensions.
- Produce the virtualisation workload architecture for {{services_virtualization_workloads}} workloads.
- Produce the integration design for {{services_integrations}} integration points.
- Produce the user-persona / role and access model design covering {{services_roles}} roles.

### Out-of-scope

- Any architecture or diagram carried forward from a prior engagement without validation against the current customer's environment.
- Any diagram that names or depicts the customer's specific environment, application, or architecture from a prior engagement.
- Any diagram or architecture for components not listed in the Phase 1 in-scope above.

### Deliverables

- Architecture design document.
- {{services_arch_diagrams}} architecture diagrams.
- Architecture review sign-off record.

## Global Assumptions

- The numbers in the master scope-numbers table are confirmed per engagement and documented in the design stage; they are not carried forward from a prior engagement without validation.
- Architecture is completed and signed off before detailed design begins; detailed design is completed and signed off before implementation begins.
- The customer provides the access, credentials, and environment information required for architecture, design, and implementation.
- The customer procures its own hardware, software, licences, and connectivity unless explicitly included in this scope.
- Implementation is performed remotely; on-site work is excluded unless explicitly added by change request.
- Implementation applies only to systems and configurations meeting the requirements defined in the signed-off design.
- The customer's technical team is available and dedicated throughout implementation as needed.
- The customer holds a full backup of the implemented solutions from the installation date onward.
- Timeline depends on customer responsiveness, access, and delivery of prerequisites.
- Where the engagement spans multiple operating locations, the customer confirms licensing, telephony permissions, and any cross-border data transfer approval per location before design sign-off.
- No security policy or firewall blocks required communication or replication between servers.
- Any modification or additional requirement beyond the stated scope is handled through a change request.
- Scope is limited to the solutions mentioned in this document and not any other solution, even those in the same portfolio.
- Services that include configured, installed, upgraded, or implemented software are not subject to customer acceptance.

## Global Out-of-Scope

- Hardware, network, and storage procurement, supply, mounting, and cabling — including for backend management and monitoring — except devices delivered by Verto Wave.
- Any civil or electrical work, or resolving power-connectivity availability from UPS to proceed with implementation.
- Any activity at branches / sites including delivery and mounting of devices.
- Any field support activities, on-site or field operations, or logistical work and direct communication with the end user.
- Any rework related to hardware availability or hardware failure issues, network connectivity issues, electricity instability, or electricity outages.
- Customs clearance, import permits, duties, in-country logistics, and equipment delivery to site.
- Procurement, supply, resale, contracting, or funding of any connectivity service — internet, fiber, DSL, satellite, cellular, MPLS, or any other wide-area transport — at any site or in the central data center. All access services are contracted directly by the customer with its chosen providers.
- Any communication, negotiation, fault escalation, or SLA management with ISPs, carriers, or satellite operators on the customer's behalf.
- Any responsibility for the availability, throughput, latency, or quality of customer-provided access services, and any service credit arising from their failure.
- Design, survey, installation, or upgrade of last-mile access, including satellite terminal installation, alignment, and commissioning.
- Obtaining telecommunications, telephony numbering, VoIP, or data-service licences and regulatory permits in any country of operation.
- The customer's core application — its design, supply, configuration, licensing, and any application data migration.
- Any validation, certification, or regulatory approval of the customer's service that is the customer's or a third party's responsibility.
- Any backup, network, and security activities not explicitly in scope.
- Any disaster-recovery activities unless the backup and continuity service is selected and separately quoted.
- Security penetration testing, vulnerability assessment, code review, and forensics activities — unless explicitly stated as in scope.
- Any deployment or configuration of third-party systems, products, or applications not included in this proposal scope.
- Integration with components that do not support standard protocols used by DeviceX/SDX.
- Integration with end-of-life / end-of-support systems and solutions.
- Creating new SLAs and agreements with vendors for systems aside from those explicitly included in this scope.
- Operating third-party systems aside from the systems included in this scope.
- Managing and supporting systems and devices not allowing ways of integration.
- Any configuration or troubleshooting of business virtual machines hosted on DeviceX, unless explicitly in scope.
- Deploying any software on created virtual machines unless explicitly in scope.
- Setting up or upgrading to newer versions of OS or database unless explicitly in scope.
- Application compatibility checks or troubleshooting with operating systems.
- Any migration activities.
- Any change to existing customer devices or configurations — switches, routers, existing firewalls, and similar.
- Any design changes to the existing environment.
- Procurement of any extra licences not identified as in-scope.
- Acquisition of any necessary commercial certificates unless explicitly in scope.
- Any documentation other than the design documents and agreed deliverables.
- Any development and debugging activities not explicitly in scope.
- Any activity not explicitly mentioned in the in-scope activities above.
- Any implementation, deployment, rollout, testing, or validation for anything not explicitly listed in the in-scope activities above.
