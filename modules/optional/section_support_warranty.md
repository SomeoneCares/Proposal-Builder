## Software Support and Maintenance

The DeviceX and StackX licenses in this proposal include support and maintenance for {{support_term_months}} months from license delivery, covering:

[[figure: support-coverage | Support coverage and response path]]


- Software updates and new releases of the licensed modules
- Security patches and fixes
- Technical support for the licensed software through the Verto Wave service desk
- Access to product documentation and known-error information

## Hardware Warranty <!-- if devicex -->

<!-- if devicex -->
DeviceX appliances are covered for {{hardware_warranty_months}} months against manufacturing defects, including replacement of defective hardware.

1. The fault is reported to the Verto Wave service desk and confirmed through remote diagnostics.
2. A replacement unit is dispatched under the agreed commercial terms.
3. The replacement joins the network through zero-touch provisioning and receives its site's configuration and policies automatically.
4. The defective unit is returned; any data on it is unreadable without its hardware-sealed keys.
<!-- endif -->

## Support Coverage

- Coverage hours: {{support_coverage}}
- Requests are raised with the Verto Wave service desk by phone or email.
- Support requests are handled under the Operations Level Agreement for as long as managed services are in place. <!-- if managed_services -->

| Severity 4 | Questions, minor issues and service requests | 4 business hours |

## Premier Support <!-- if premier_support -->

<!-- if premier_support -->
Verto Wave provides Premier Support visits during the first year:

- First six months: one on-site visit per month
- Second six months: one on-site visit per quarter

Each visit covers:

- Updating all licensed modules to the latest supported version
- A health check of all modules and resolution of any issues found
- Implementing any advanced configuration required
<!-- endif -->

## Support Exclusions

- Support for software versions that are no longer supported, and for modules not licensed under this proposal
- Hardware damage caused by misuse, environmental conditions or power events <!-- if devicex -->
- Faults caused by changes not made or approved by Verto Wave
