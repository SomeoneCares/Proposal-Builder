## How We Engage

1. **Understanding the requirement** — structured discussions and assessments identify {{customer_short}}'s needs, constraints and the gaps the engagement has to close.
2. **Envisioning the current environment** — a review of the systems already in place gives a complete picture of the technical landscape before anything is designed.
3. **Proposing a value-added solution** — the design is built around those findings rather than a standard pattern, and states the outcome each element delivers.
4. **Seeking {{customer_short}}'s feedback** — the proposed architecture is reviewed with {{customer_short}} and adjusted until it matches the requirement.
5. **Beyond completion** — the close of implementation begins the operating relationship, with continuous improvement driven by what the platform reports. <!-- if managed_services -->

## Engagement Functions

| Function | Responsibility |
| :--- | :--- |
| Engagement management team | Owns the engagement across its lifecycle: communication, coordination and alignment between {{customer_short}}'s stakeholders and the Verto Wave technical teams, and accountability for the agreed outcomes. |
| Project management and governance office | Provides governance, oversight and standard practice across Verto Wave engagements, so each stage completes against its criteria and reporting stays consistent. |
| Technical leadership | Owns the technical design and the build team's work, and carries the design decisions into operations. <!-- if services --> |
| Operations management | Owns steady-state service performance against the Operations Level Agreement and the improvement plan. <!-- if managed_services --> |
