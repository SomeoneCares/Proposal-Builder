## Company Profile

[[figure: about-vertowave | Verto Wave at a glance]]


Verto Wave brings over 25 years of expertise and more than 500 successful engagements to its customers. The company helps organizations strategize, build and operate their IT, paving the road for digital transformation and sustainable growth.

Verto Wave works across three pillars:

| Pillar | What it means for customers |
| :--- | :--- |
| Strategize — plan for success | Developing digital strategies and raising customers' IT maturity. |
| Build — bring ideas to life | Delivering a comprehensive business-service observability, control and governance stack. |
| Operate — sustain and grow | Operating the stack with precision to ensure peak performance and risk mitigation. |

## Sectors Served

Verto Wave serves customers in the financial, healthcare, corporate, public and telecommunications sectors.

## Solution Portfolio

- DeviceX/SDX converged edge platform — networking, security and application hosting at each site
- StackX — observability, automation, service management, identity, resilience and security operations
- Configuration, systems and network management
- IT service management and asset management
- Automation and orchestration, DevOps and business service mapping

## Our Values

- **Customer-focused solutions** — designs built around each customer's environment and goals.
- **Commitment to quality** — a disciplined delivery methodology and verified outcomes.
- **Continuous improvement** — lessons from every engagement feed the next.
- **Results-driven approach** — success measured by our customers' operational results.
